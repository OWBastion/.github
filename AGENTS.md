# Agent guidance for `.github`

This repository owns stable organization-wide engineering policy and routing. Use [`README.md`](README.md) as the canonical index and load only the policy relevant to the task.

When editing organization guidance, keep repository-specific contracts with their owning repositories and mutable execution state with its live owner. Do not create a second policy store in `.agents` or `overwatch-ai-skills`; their separate procedure and public-skill roles are described in the README.
